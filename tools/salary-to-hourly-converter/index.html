Common questions

      

        Am I underpaid?
        
Compare your hourly rate to freelancers doing your work. If a freelancer charges 2x your hourly, you're leaving money on the table (or getting big benefits/security in return).

      

      

        Should I quit and freelance?
        
Only if you can bill 25+ hours/week at 2x your current hourly. Below that, salary + benefits usually wins on total compensation.

      

      

        What's a fair rate for a senior role?
        
US software engineer avg $65-100/hr. UK £40-70/hr. Depends on location, experience, and specialty.
