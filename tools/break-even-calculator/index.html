Common questions

      

        What is contribution margin?
        
Price minus variable cost per unit — what each sale contributes toward covering fixed costs after that unit's own cost.

      

      

        How often should I recalculate break-even?
        
Every time fixed costs or pricing changes. Quarterly at minimum. It's the difference between profitable and quietly losing money.

      

      

        My break-even is impossible. What now?
        
Your variable cost is higher than your price. Raise the price, cut the variable cost, or drop the product. Selling more never fixes negative unit economics.
