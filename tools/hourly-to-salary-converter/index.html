Common questions

      

        How many weeks do I actually work?
        
50 weeks assuming 2 weeks unpaid vacation. Adjust down for sick days, holidays, unpaid time off.

      

      

        Is 40 hours realistic?
        
For employees yes. For freelancers/contractors, 25-30 BILLABLE hours is more accurate. The rest goes to admin, sales, unpaid work.

      

      

        Should I include benefits value?
        
For total comp comparison yes. Healthcare, 401k match, PTO can add 25-40% to a salary's true value.
