Common questions

      

        When is simple interest used?
        
Short-term loans, some auto loans, and treasury bills. Rare for savings accounts, which almost always compound.

      

      

        How do I convert an annual rate to monthly?
        
For simple interest, monthly rate = annual rate ÷ 12. For compound, it's different.

      

      

        Is simple interest better for the borrower?
        
Yes — pays less than compound interest for the same principal and rate.
