Common questions

      

        What's the standard format for invoice numbers?
        
Most businesses use PREFIX-YEAR-NUMBER (like INV-2026-0001). Sequential numbering is a legal requirement in most countries and helps you track invoices in order.

      

      

        Can I reset my invoice numbers every year?
        
Yes. Many businesses restart the counter each January with the year in the middle: INV-2026-0001, INV-2027-0001. Both patterns are accepted by tax authorities.

      

      

        Do invoice numbers have to be sequential?
        
In most tax systems yes — sequential without gaps. If you cancel an invoice, don't reuse the number; issue a credit note instead.
