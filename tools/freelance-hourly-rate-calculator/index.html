Common questions

      

        Most freelancers underprice — why?
        
They calculate rate = desired salary / 2000 hours. But you can't bill 40 hours a week — 20-25 is realistic. And you owe taxes, expenses, and unpaid time. Real rate is 2-3x what most freelancers charge.

      

      

        What % of my week is actually billable?
        
Successful freelancers hit 50-65% billable time. Rest goes to admin, sales, learning, sick days. Assume 25 billable hours/week max.

      

      

        Should I raise my rate on existing clients?
        
Yes, once a year. 5-10%. Announce 60 days ahead. Most clients don't leave — they expect it.
