Common questions

      

        How do I calculate a discount percentage from two prices?
        
((Original − Final) / Original) × 100. Example: 500 down to 425 = 75/500 = 15% off.

      

      

        Should I offer percentage or flat-amount discounts?
        
Percentages read as more generous on high-ticket items. Flat amounts feel bigger on low-ticket items. Test both.

      

      

        How do I calculate multiple stacked discounts?
        
Apply one after the other, not by adding. 20% then 10% is NOT 30% off — it's 28% off (0.8 × 0.9 = 0.72).
