Common questions

      

        Does putting late-fee terms on the invoice make them enforceable?
        
In most jurisdictions yes — if they are visible before the customer accepts the work. The safest place is on the quote or contract, then repeated on the invoice.

      

      

        Can I add interest even if the customer never agreed?
        
Usually no — courts require prior notice. That's why the terms line matters. Once it's on your invoice, future work counts as accepted.

      

      

        Should I offer early-payment discounts?
        
Common in B2B — '2/10 Net 30' means 2% off if paid in 10 days, otherwise due in 30. Effective for slow-paying enterprise clients.
