Common questions

      

        What's a good SaaS churn rate?
        
Under 5% monthly for early stage. Under 2% monthly for mature B2C. Under 1% monthly for B2B enterprise. Above 5% = leaky bucket.

      

      

        Is annual churn just 12x monthly?
        
No — it compounds. 5% monthly churn = 46% annual churn (not 60%). Use the compounded formula.

      

      

        How do I reduce churn?
        
In order: fix the reason people quit (talk to churned users), onboarding, product-market fit, customer success touches. Discounts to save customers rarely work long-term.
